Micro-Duty Cycle Scheduling Explained

Silicon chips heat up exponentially during continuous uninterrupted compute. By applying micro-bursts of SIGSTOP/SIGCONT (or NtSuspendProcess), heat dissipates immediately without hurting token stream perception.

COOL (300ms)
